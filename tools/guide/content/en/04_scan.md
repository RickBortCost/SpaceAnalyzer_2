# 4. Scanning and choosing signals

![scan](«Address Space · scan» panel (left column of the LIVE page).)

| N. | Control |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Start node**: root of the navigation (`i=85` = Objects). |
| 2 | **Node limit** per scan (default 50,000). |
| 3 | **Browse branches**: opens the tree one branch at a time, useful on very large servers. |
| 4 | **Scan all**: walks the whole address space (hundreds of nodes in the example, 0 errors) with progress bar and Interrupt button. |
| 5 | **Scan list / Tree**: two ways of browsing the results. |
| 6 | **Search** by name, path or NodeId. |
| 7 | **Variables only**: hides folders and objects. |
| 8 | **Select results**: adds all filtered variables to the trend. |

A single row can also be ticked («Chart») or a NodeId typed by hand in «Add NodeId / management». Selected signals appear in the «Live signals» column with current value, quality and type. Only numeric variables enter the trend; the others can still be inspected and recorded. Up to 64 signals in total. During Record the selection stays fixed until Stop.
